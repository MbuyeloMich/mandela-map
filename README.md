## About Nelson Mandela 🇿🇦

Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela (1918-2013) was one of history's most influential leaders and freedom fighters. From his early days as a young activist to his 27 years of imprisonment on Robben Island, from his pivotal role in ending apartheid to becoming South Africa's first Black president—his life is a testament to the power of persistence, forgiveness, and the human spirit.

This interactive map traces his extraordinary 95-year journey across South Africa and the world, documenting 45 pivotal moments that shaped not just a nation, but humanity's understanding of freedom and equality.

**What You're Looking At:** Every dot on this map represents a turning point. Every movement tells a story of struggle, hope, and transformation. From the youth days in Johannesburg to his release from prison, from his presidency to his continued humanitarian work—this is Mandela's life, mapped.

**Live Demo:** Open `index.html` in your browser and walk through history with one of humanity's greatest leaders.

## 💡 Why This Map Exists

This interactive map transforms Mandela's life from abstract history into something visceral and real. You don't just *read* that he was imprisoned for 27 years—you *see* Robben Island on the map and understand the geographic isolation of his punishment. You don't just *learn* he became president—you track his entire journey to that moment.

**The Technical Why:** We took a trajectory mapping framework and dedicated it to one of history's most important figures because his story deserves to be told in a way that engages people—where geography, timeline, and personal narrative all converge.

**The Human Why:** Because Nelson Mandela showed us that one person's commitment to justice, forgiveness, and human dignity can reshape the world. This map is a tribute to that legacy.

## The 95 Events You'll Explore

From **1918** (birth in rural Transkei) to **2013** (his final years in Johannesburg), this map documents every major milestone across Mandela's extraordinary 95-year life:

- His early activism and founding of the ANC Youth League
- His famous Rivonia Trial and conviction (1964)
- His 27 years imprisoned on Robben Island
- His release and role in ending apartheid
- His historic presidency (1994-1999)
- His continued humanitarian work until his death

Each event is geolocated, dated, and contextualized. This isn't just a timeline—it's a geographic narrative of one man's fight for freedom.
